Section 01

Vision & Strategy

Forge is a cross-platform mobile and web product that helps independent personal trainers, multi-coach studios, and their clients run training as a real business. The brand promise: you are not just training, you are forging yourself. The PT side handles programming, scheduling, billing, and client communication. The client side handles workout logging, progress, nutrition, and motivation. A management layer (v3) lets a head coach oversee other PTs working under their brand, with revenue tracking and check-in/check-out billing.

Launch geography

Lebanon first, then UAE. Two single-timezone countries. USD in v1, AED in v2. Stripe is unavailable to Lebanese businesses, so v1 payments run on Whish (mobile wallet) and Areeba (cards). Stripe Connect arrives in v2 alongside the UAE expansion.

Operating thesis

PT-first, not consumer-first. The hardest market to win is solo trainers running their business on WhatsApp and Excel. If Forge saves them admin hours, they bring their clients with them — a much cheaper acquisition curve than competing with consumer fitness apps.

The three bets

1. Logging must be faster than a notebook (≤2 taps, sub-second, offline).
2. Billing must just work — failed cards, refunds, no-shows, packs.
3. AI must save time in week one — drafts programs and recaps, never adds a task.

What v1 deliberately does NOT ship

  • AI Avatar Trainer — real-time CV form-correction is a v2 problem; v1 stays with text-prompted AI drafts.
  • Wearables (Apple Health, Garmin, Whoop, Oura) — deferred to v3. In-person PT-led use case doesn't need them day one.
  • 1:1 messaging & video calls — deferred to v2. v1 ships announcements only (one-way, ≤500 chars, multi-recipient).
  • Multi-coach studios — deferred to v3. Lebanon supply is dominated by solo PTs; multi-coach matters after UAE.
  • Tax handling — explicitly out of scope. Receipts show price only; PTs handle their own tax obligations.
  • Stripe Connect — v2 only. Lebanon launches on Whish + Areeba.
  • Group classes & marketplace discovery — v2 / v3.

Section 02

Personas & Permissions

Four personas drive permissions and UX choices throughout the spec. The PT is a single account that operates in one or more modes (Solo, Master, Sub-under-Master) on the same login; the data model keeps each mode's clients, sessions, and subscription separated. Gym Account is always free, no card, no down payment.

PT — Solo / Master / Sub modes

Primary actor. Single Forge login. Operates in one or more modes simultaneously:

  • Solo mode — independent contractor, owns clients, pays own Solo tier (Free trial / Pro / Elite / Power).
  • Master mode — has 1+ sub-PTs. Pays Master tier covering sub-PT seats + combined client pool. Sees aggregated view of all sub-PT clients.
  • Sub-under-Master mode — connected to a Master. App access covered by master's sub. Sees only master-assigned clients. May also run Solo mode on the same login.

Client

Trains 1-5×/week. Mix of in-person and online. Free if invited by a PT (counts against PT's plan cap). $4.99/mo Premium if self-serve with no PT. Pain: motivation gaps, unclear progress. Wants: clear next workout, visible progress.

Gym Account v1

Gym entity — always free, no card required, no down payment. v1 ships single shared login. Purpose: PT roster management, gym-subbed (offline) client list, calendar/space booking, gym profile/branding, analytics on counts. Never sees PT-owned client progress — only anonymous counts. Marketplace + events deferred to Phase B/C.

Platform Admin

Forge-internal support, fraud, operations. Only role with visibility into subscription billing data. Read-only by default; write access gated by permission.

Collapsed personas: the previous Owner / Studio Owner persona is replaced by Gym Account (free) + Master PT (paid mode). Sub-Coach / Junior PT is renamed to Sub-PT under Master PT — a PT-under-PT relationship, not PT-under-gym. Studio Admin is folded into the Gym Account single login for v1; multi-staff roles deferred to v2+.

Section 03

Product Pillars

Every feature in the spec maps to one of six pillars. They are how product decisions are framed, prioritized, and measured.

P1

Logging & Tracking

The fastest, most reliable way in the world to record what happened in a workout. Sub-second log-a-set, offline-first, voice-friendly.

P2

Programming & Nutrition

Build a 4-, 8-, or 12-week training plan in minutes. Pair it with a meal plan that respects macros and dietary constraints.

P3

Coaching & Communication

Announcements in v1; messaging, video, and form-check video review in v2. Automated nudges, a real relationship loop.

P4

Business & Scheduling

Schedules, check-in / check-out, capacity caps per subscription tier. No inter-party payments — packages, memberships, no-show fees, and coach payouts are out of scope. Forge subscription billing visible only to Platform Admin.

P5

Insights & AI

Charts and reports the client and PT actually open. AI that drafts work, summarizes work, and flags risk — not a chatbot novelty.

P6

Marketplace & Growth

Discovery for clients to find coaches (v3). Branded portals so coaches market their own brand. Referral rewards, community challenges, Forge Shop.

Section 05

System Architecture

Three clients fan into a single API. Stateless app tier with PostgreSQL primary and Redis for cache/queues. Object store for media. Payments and video are managed services. AI is brokered through a service that scrubs PII before calling the LLM. Multi-AZ from day one.

Recommended stack
MobileReact Native (Expo bare workflow)
WebNext.js 14+ (App Router), React, TypeScript, shared types with API
APINode.js + TypeScript (NestJS or Fastify), tRPC or GraphQL
DB / Cache / ObjectPostgreSQL · Redis · S3-compatible object store
RealtimeManaged WebSocket (Pusher, Ably, Centrifugo)
Video (v2)LiveKit or Daily — do not roll your own SFU
Paymentsv1 Whish + Areeba; v2 Stripe Connect Express
AIAnthropic Claude (managed). Pose (v2): MediaPipe / MoveNet on-device
AuthAuth0, Clerk, or self-hosted Keycloak. Argon2id. JWT short-lived + rotating refresh
PushAPNs + FCM via abstraction (Expo Notifications or OneSignal)
InfraAWS (ECS/Fargate or EKS), Terraform, GitHub Actions, multi-AZ

Section 11

Monetization

Single payment direction: PT and Client → Forge. Forge is always the merchant of record. No client→PT, no gym→PT, no PT→PT in-app payments. Solo PT tiers (existing) are kept; Master PT tier is new and bundles sub-PT seats. Gym Account is always free. AI is sold as credit packs. No tax collected or remitted by Forge.

Section 14

Glossary

The terms used throughout this document. Memorize the abbreviations — they appear in every section.

1RM
One-rep max. Maximum weight a person can lift for a single repetition of a given lift.
Adherence score
0–100 metric per client per day, factoring logged sessions vs programmed, macros vs target, body metrics frequency.
AED
UAE dirham. v2 currency.
AMRAP
"As many reps as possible". A set scheme where the lifter does max reps at a fixed load.
Areeba
Lebanese card processor handling Visa / Mastercard via hosted page. v1 card rails.
ASVS
OWASP Application Security Verification Standard. Forge target: Level 2 for API.
Block
Sub-unit of a programming Day — a grouping of supersetted or paired Exercises.
COD
Cash on delivery. First-class checkout option in the Forge Shop.
Credit pack
Pre-paid bundle of AI generations. 1 credit = 1 program/meal plan/recap.
Deload
Programmed lower-intensity week to allow recovery.
DIFC / ADGM
Dubai International Financial Centre / Abu Dhabi Global Market. Common UAE free-zone jurisdictions for regional startups.
E2E
End-to-end encryption (e.g., progress photos with separate key envelope).
Forge Shop
First-party marketplace of fitness essentials (EP-21). Deferred to v3+.
Gym Account
Free, non-billable entity. Hosts PT roster, gym-subbed (offline) client list, calendar, gym profile/branding. v1 ships single shared login. Never sees PT-owned client progress — only counts.
Gym-subbed client
Offline gym member entered by a Gym Account. Named to the gym, no progress data tied unless they also become a PT-linked client.
GymMembership
Junction entity between PT and Gym. Many-to-many. Carries join-state (invited / requested / active / left) and who initiated.
IAP
In-app purchase. Apple / Google billing rails for digital goods (AI credit packs, Premium).
KMS
Key management service (AWS KMS or equivalent). Holds encryption keys with rotation ≤90 days.
LBP
Lebanese pound. Not supported in any Forge version — USD only in Lebanon.
Live mirror
Real-time reflection of PT-side logging on the Client's device, ≤3s push (F-071).
MASVS
OWASP Mobile Application Security Verification Standard. Forge target: Level 2.
MFA
Multi-factor authentication. TOTP or SMS in Forge.
Master PT
PT mode where one or more Sub-PTs operate under the master. Pays a Master tier (Pro $40 / Elite $99 / Power $199) that covers sub-PT seats + a combined client pool. Sees aggregated view of all sub-PT clients.
PAR-Q
Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire. Standard pre-training health screen.
PCI SAQ-A
Lowest PCI DSS scope — applies when no PAN data touches your servers (hosted payment pages only).
Periodization
Long-arc structuring of training intensity / volume. Linear, undulating, block models.
PHI
Protected health information under HIPAA. Forge avoids in v1.
PII
Personally identifiable information. Scrubbed before broker → LLM call.
RBAC
Role-based access control. Drives the v1/v3 permission matrix.
RPE
Rate of perceived exertion (1–10). Logged per set; feeds AI suggestions.
RTL
Right-to-left layout. Required from day one for Arabic.
SFU
Selective Forwarding Unit. WebRTC architecture for group video — use LiveKit / Daily, do not roll your own.
SLA
Service-level agreement. GDPR export ≤24h; receipts ≤60s of check-out.
SLO
Service-level objective. Internal targets behind the SLAs.
PT mode
One of Solo / Master / Sub-under-Master. A single Forge account can carry one or more active modes simultaneously; the data model keeps each mode's clients, sessions, and subscription separated.
PT-linked client
Client invited by a PT (Solo, Master, or Sub). Free under the PT's plan cap. Visible to the PT; anonymous count only to any linked gym.
Sub-PT (under Master)
PT mode connected to a Master PT. App access covered by master's subscription. Sees only master-assigned clients. May simultaneously hold own Solo mode on the same login.
ULID
Universally Unique Lexicographically Sortable Identifier. Client-side ID for Sets that guarantees conflict-free ordering across offline + sync.
USD
US dollar. v1 unit of account. Hedges Lebanese banking volatility.
WCAG 2.2 AA
Web Content Accessibility Guidelines target level. Forge baseline.
Wakkilni
Lebanese last-mile delivery service. Forge Shop fulfilment in v2.
Whish
Lebanese mobile-wallet payments. Whish Money. v1 wallet rail.
